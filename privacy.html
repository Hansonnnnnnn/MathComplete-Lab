Privacy Policy

Draft effective July 17, 2026. This policy must be reviewed by the site operator before production launch.

Information collected

Guest practice records, theme, language, and preferences may be stored in your browser. If you create an account, the service stores your email, display name, consent record, sign-in identities, practice attempts, mistakes, favorites, and security settings.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate you, synchronize progress, show learning history, protect the service, respond to account actions, and let you export or delete your data. It is not sold for advertising.

Service providers

Supabase provides authentication and database services. Google may provide social sign-in, Cloudflare Turnstile may prevent abuse, and Resend may deliver account email. Their processing is governed by their own policies and the operator's configuration.

Storage and retention

Guest records remain on the current device until browser data is cleared. Account records remain until you delete the account or the operator must retain limited information for security or legal obligations.

Security and choices

The site uses row-level database policies, verified sessions, optional MFA, and encrypted network transport. You can download your account data, change account details, revoke sessions, and permanently delete the account from Account Settings.

Users aged 13 and older

The service is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly create accounts for them.

Contact

Before production launch, the operator must add a monitored privacy contact and applicable regional disclosures here.

隐私政策

草案生效日期：2026 年 7 月 17 日。正式上线前须由网站运营者审核。

收集的信息

访客练习记录、主题、语言和偏好可能保存在浏览器中。创建账户后，本站会保存邮箱、显示名称、同意记录、登录身份、答题记录、错题、收藏和安全设置。

信息用途

信息用于身份验证、同步进度、展示学习历史、保护服务、处理账户操作，以及支持数据导出和删除。本站不会为广告目的出售这些信息。

服务提供商

Supabase 提供认证与数据库服务；Google 可提供社交登录；Cloudflare Turnstile 可用于防止滥用；Resend 可发送账户邮件。相关处理同时受这些服务的政策和运营者配置约束。

存储与保留

访客记录保留在当前设备，直到浏览器数据被清除。账户记录通常保留至账户被删除；因安全或法律义务需要时，运营者可能保留有限信息。

安全与选择

本站采用数据库行级权限、已验证会话、可选 MFA 和加密网络传输。你可以在账户设置中下载数据、更改资料、撤销会话或永久删除账户。

仅面向 13 岁以上用户

本站不面向未满 13 岁的儿童，也不会在知情情况下为其创建账户。

联系方式

正式上线前，运营者需要在此补充受监控的隐私联系邮箱和适用地区披露。
